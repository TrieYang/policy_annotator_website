| System Overview | Machine learning-based resume screening system that analyzes job applications using NLP models to evaluate candidate qualifications, experience, and skills matching. Integrates with major ATS platforms and provides automated ranking, keyword analysis, and qualification matching through REST APIs. |
| Primary intended uses | (1) Resume Screening: Automatically evaluate resumes against job requirements; (2) Candidate Ranking: Score and rank applicants based on job relevance; (3) Skills Gap Analysis: Identify missing qualifications versus job requirements; (4) Keyword Extraction: Highlight relevant skills and experiences |
| Primary intended users | (1) HR Professionals: Screening large volumes of applications; (2) Recruitment Teams: Managing candidate pipelines; (3) Hiring Managers: Reviewing pre-screened candidates; (4) Corporate HR Departments: Standardizing hiring processes |
| Out-of-scope use cases | (1) Background Checks: Not designed for verification of employment history or credentials; (2) Personality Assessment: Cannot evaluate behavioral traits or cultural fit; (3) Video/Image Analysis: Not capable of processing visual media or video resumes; (4) Direct Hiring Decisions: Should not be used as sole decision maker for hiring |

| Dataset Description | Training dataset includes 5 million anonymized resumes across 20 industries, covering entry-level to executive positions, with balanced representation across demographics |
| Collection Method | (1) Licensed Resume Databases: Purchased from professional job boards; (2) Partner Organizations: Contributed anonymized historical data; (3) Public Job Applications: With explicit consent for AI training; (4) Synthetic Data Generation: For underrepresented job categories |

| Actions taken | (1) Bias Detection: Implementation of regular bias audits across protected characteristics; (2) Language Neutrality: Integration of multilingual processing capabilities; (3) Diverse Training Data: Expanded training dataset to include varied career paths and formats; (4) Regular Calibration: Monthly adjustments based on hiring outcome feedback |

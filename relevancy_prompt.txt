### **What is Compliance Contribution Score?**
The **Compliance Contribution Score** evaluates how necessary a section is for assessing compliance with the provided legal document. Instead of measuring how "useful" or "related" a section is, it measures the impact of removing that section on the ability to determine compliance.

It is scored based on **how much the absence of this section would hinder compliance assessment** for this model card, rather than how informative it is when present. 

- **Level 0 (Not Needed)**: The section has no impact on compliance assessment. Removing it does not affect this model card's ability to determine compliance to one or multiple policy paragraphs.
- **Level 5 (Essential)**: The section is indispensable. Without it, determining compliance to one or multiple policy paragraphs would be impossible or highly unreliable.
- **Levels 1-4 (Partially Contributory)**: The section provides some information useful for compliance assessment, but its removal would not entirely prevent compliance determination to one or multiple policy paragraphs.

### **Metrics**
#### Violation Level (0–5)
- **Level 0 – No Violation**: does not violate the article's requirements.
- **Level 1 – Minor Gaps**: Minor issues or lack of detail, but mostly compliant.
- **Level 2 – Partial Violation**: Noticeable non-compliance with some key expectations missing.
- **Level 3 – Moderate Violation**: Multiple gaps or inconsistencies with the article.
- **Level 4 – Major Violation**: Largely contradicts the article’s requirements or omits critical elements.
- **Level 5 – Severe Violation**: Completely fails to meet the article’s requirements and may introduce risk or legal non-compliance.

#### **Compliance Contribution Score** (0-5)  
- **Level 0 - Not Needed**: The section has no impact on compliance assessment. Removing it does not affect this model card's ability to determine compliance to one or multiple policy paragraphs.
- **Level 1 - Marginal Contribution**: Provides minimal information, with little impact to determine compliance to one or multiple policy paragraphs if removed.  
- **Level 2 - Some Contribution**: Offers indirect relevance but does not directly determine compliance, or content applicable but jurisdiction out of scope(for example the region doesn't apply)  
- **Level 3 - Moderate Contribution**: Provides partial but significant evidence that helps assess compliance.  
- **Level 4 - High Contribution**: The section is important for compliance assessment and removing it would make evaluation difficult.  
- **Level 5 - Essential Contribution**: The section is indispensable. Without it, determining compliance to one or multiple policy paragraphs would be **impossible or highly unreliable**.

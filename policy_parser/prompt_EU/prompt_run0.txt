### Metrics
Compliance Level:
Level 0 – Non-Compliant: The section directly contradicts legal requirements or provides no relevant compliance information.
Level 1 – Severely Insufficient: The section contains very little or vague information that does not support compliance assessment.
Level 2 – Partially Compliant: Some elements align with the legal document, but major gaps exist in evidence or implementation details.
Level 3 – Moderately Compliant: The section provides partial compliance, covering key requirements but lacking full implementation or supporting evidence.
Level 4 – Largely Compliant: The section satisfies most compliance requirements with clear evidence but has minor gaps or missing details.
Level 5 – Fully Compliant: The section completely adheres to the provided legal document’s requirements with detailed evidence, proper implementation, and no compliance gaps.

Relevance Level:
Level 0 - Not Relevant: The information in this section has no bearing on evaluating compliance with provided legal documents. It contains details that cannot be used to assess any aspect of the risk management requirements.
Level 1 - Marginally Relevant: The section contains information that has very limited utility for compliance assessment. While it may provide some context about the AI system, it offers minimal insight into risk management practices or compliance with the provided legal document.
Level 2 - Somewhat Relevant: The information has indirect relevance to compliance assessment. It provides background or contextual information that helps understand the AI system but offers limited direct evidence for evaluating compliance with the provided legal document.
Level 3 - Moderately Relevant: The section contains information that is useful for assessing compliance with some aspects of provided legal document. It provides partial evidence or indicators that can be used to evaluate certain risk management requirements, though not comprehensively.
Level 4 - Highly Relevant: The information is directly applicable to evaluating compliance with multiple key requirements of the provided legal requirement. It provides clear evidence that can be used to assess specific aspects of its policy compliance with good detail.
Level 5 - Critically Relevant: The section contains information that is essential and central to evaluating compliance with the provided legal document. It provides direct, detailed evidence that directly corresponds to specific requirements of the legal document and is indispensable for a thorough compliance assessment.
